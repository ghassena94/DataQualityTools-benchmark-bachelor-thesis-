# Fable review of "Block D final plan v2" (9 Oct 2026)

**Verdict: ready after fixes.** The measured tables, the flag replay, the sign-flip test and the merged permutation test all hold up under independent recomputation. What does not hold up is the prediction section (§9) and three "= floor" / "2 cells" statements that the driver's hash logic and the impute prediction rest on, plus two of the eleven REIN corrections, which introduce new errors. Nothing stops the driver from being built; the prediction must be rewritten before it is committed (Sat 10 Oct, step 1).

Severity: **blocker** = must change before the prediction is committed; **must-fix** = before the first analysis is read; **should-fix** = before the write-up; **minor** = wording.

All numbers below were recomputed with my own code in the rein container (pandas 1.3.3, scikit-learn 0.24.2, SciPy 1.7.1, xgboost 1.3.3), read-only on `rein-datasets/`. Scripts and raw outputs are in this folder:
`fable_measure.py` / `fable_measure_output.txt` / `fable_measure.json` (flag replay with the real great_expectations library and an own DC-violation implementation, per-seed rows kept, references, impute-vs-floor matrices), `fable_check2.py` / `fable_check2_output.txt` (impute-vs-floor magnitudes and S2 score differences; adult HoloClean cell identity), `fable_wilcoxon.py` / `fable_wilcoxon_output.txt`, `fable_typeI_sim.py` / `fable_typeI_sim_output.txt`, `fable_implied_concordance.txt`, `fable_giant_typos.py` / `fable_giant_typos_output.txt`.

## Findings

### Blocker

**B1. §9 predicts orders that §8 never scores, and predictions 1–2 contradict prediction 7 under the primary reference.**
- §4 and §9.1–9.3 say the downstream order "follows recall" (GT repair), "row-level precision" (delete) and "precision on parsable errors" (impute). None of these is one of the three references in §8.3; the concordance machinery scores pairs only against Table 3 cell F1 (a), row F1 (b) and model-visible F1 (c). So predictions 1–3 cannot be marked right or wrong by the analysis script as specified.
- Against reference (a), the recall order is a different order on every dataset (`fable_measure_output.txt`, `ref recall_model`): bc HoloClean 0.794 > hand-written 0.678 > Raha 0.552 (Table 3 puts HoloClean last); nasa Raha 1.000 > hand-written 0.930; bike Raha 0.939 > HoloClean 0.763 > hand-written 0.703; adult HoloClean 0.986 > Raha 0.509.
- If §4's mechanisms hold exactly, the pooled concordance against (a) is (`fable_implied_concordance.txt`): GT repair 19 concordant / 10 discordant of 29 pairs (0.66); delete with degenerates ranked last 19 / 6 (0.76, 4 pairs undetermined by precision ties); impute (parsable-recall order) 16 / 9 (0.64). Prediction 7 ("concordance highest under ground-truth repair") is then false by the plan's own mechanism. The plan commits both to git.
- Also, most of §9.2–9.3 restates facts already fixed by the frozen flags and the split rule (which configurations are degenerate, "60 % of rows lost", "= floor" ties); these are not falsifiable by training.
- *Fix:* (i) state every prediction against the reference it will be scored on, as expected concordant/discordant pairs per dataset × policy (the table in `fable_implied_concordance.txt` is a starting point); (ii) either add the recall, row-precision and parsable-TP orders as secondary references computed from the frozen flags (cheap: they are already in `measure_fixes.json`-style counts) or drop the "follows recall/precision" wording; (iii) re-derive or drop prediction 7; (iv) move the flag-determined facts into §5 and keep in §9 only statements that training can refute.

### Must-fix

**M1. The "= floor" and "(2 cells ≠ floor)" claims (§0 fix 8, §4, §5, §9.3, §11 dedup) are wrong for nasa clean-profiled and only hold up to rounding elsewhere; a sha256 `train_hash` will not catch any of them.**
- Measured by building the floor and impute training matrices with the plan's rules (`fable_check2_output.txt`):
  - nasa GE clean-profiled under impute differs from the floor in 191–268 cells per seed with max |diff| 2.26·10¹¹, and its S2 moves against the floor by up to 0.14 RMSE (XGB), 0.013 (RF), 0.006 (Ridge). Its "2 parsable TPs" are the two thickness typos `2.25511E+11` and `2.21144E+11` (rows 1334, 1215; `fable_giant_typos_output.txt`); imputing them changes the thickness fill mean from 3.17·10⁸ to the order of 10⁷, so every unflagged unparsable thickness cell in training (≈250 per seed) is filled differently. "2 cells ≠ floor" is therefore not a near-tie, it is the single largest lever on nasa.
  - More generally nasa has 223 parsable typos more than 10 clean ranges outside the clean range (frequency 27, angle 19, chord_length 68, velocity 36, thickness 73). hand-written and Raha flag all 223, HoloClean 185, clean-profiled 2, dirty-profiled 0. Under impute and GT repair the nasa ranking is largely decided by these cells, not by the other ~500 errors. The plan's D5 argument ("pollution is a real cost of missed errors") is defensible, but §9.3 and §5 must say that this is the mechanism.
  - bc GE dirty-profiled under impute differs from the floor in 2–7 cells per seed by ≤ 3.1·10⁻¹⁵; nasa GE dirty-profiled in 71–202 cells by ≤ 6·10⁻¹⁰ (recomputed fill means; the 13 bc FPs are `?` cells, unparsable, so no value is overwritten). Scores are identical (d = 0.000 for all three models), so the *tie* prediction stands, but the training data are not "exactly the floor's", a sha256 of the float matrix differs, the §8.2 "identical hash → tie" shortcut and the §11 "skip training when train_hash repeats" dedup will not fire, and bc clean-profiled is the only version that is byte-identical (0 cells differ on 10/10 seeds).
- *Fix:* (a) replace "= floor" by "= floor up to rounding of the fill mean (scores identical)" and drop "(2 cells ≠ floor)"; (b) hash values rounded to ~12 significant digits (or compare with `np.allclose`) for the dedup and the tie shortcut; (c) treat |d| < 10⁻⁹ as zero in the pair rule; (d) complete the §4 rule: a configuration produces the floor only if *all its flagged model-column cells*, FPs included, are unparsable.

**M2. §10 item 9's replacement sentence is itself wrong.** "its test set differs per configuration" describes S1, not S5. REIN Table 3 (p. 4) defines S5 as train on Repaired, test on *Dirty*; the dirty test set is the same for every configuration. The review (B9) only asked to qualify "wrong labels". *Fix:* "No S5: REIN uses it for cleaners that output a model (p. 4, 'S5 is mainly used with ML-oriented repair methods'); its dirty test set carries wrong labels on bc (15 flips) and adult (1,701), none on nasa and bike."

**M3. §10 item 4's replacement is partly wrong and drops a true statement.** "REIN shows only S1/S4 for it [Adult]": Fig. 6a shows S1, S4 and S5 for Adult (p. 9: "Figure 6a shows the F1 score of the generated models in scenarios S1, S4, and S5 for the Adult dataset"). And the original sentence "REIN trains models on three of them (Fig. 7)" is correct: the Fig. 7 panels are (c) Adult, (d) Adult-SVC, (e) BreastCancer, (f) BreastCancer-XGB, (j) Nasa, (k) Nasa-DT; S2 appears only in (n)/(o) SoilMoisture. *Fix:* keep "REIN trains models on three of the four (Fig. 7c–f, 7j, 7k)" and add the caveat that REIN's Adult (error rate 0.58, Table 4; rule violations and outliers) is not the local adult (1,701 label flips), and that REIN reports S2 only for Soil Moisture.

**M4. Fix 2's bike number is dominated by an excluded column, and "format-only" is defined by string inequality, not value equality.**
- Of bike Raha's 13,261 "format-only" FPs, 10,931 are in `dteday`, which fix 1 removes from every policy; in model columns the count is 2,330 (windspeed 2,049, hum 279, temp 1, atemp 1) of 3,636 model-column FPs, and they cost Raha 247 training rows under delete (rows touched 16,198 → 15,951). The plan's own `measure_fixes.json` has `format_only_fp_schema = 2330`; the text uses the all-column figure. The `dteday` cells do not even parse equal (`359399.01666666666` vs `359399.0167`), so "value is correct but written differently" is not what the count measures.
- nasa is unaffected (1,864 = 1,365 frequency + 499 angle, all parse equal; HoloClean's 500 = 499 angle + 1 target cell).
- Consequence for D6: on nasa "Raha minus format-only FPs" is the perfect detector on the disk file (731 TPs, 0 FPs) and 710/731 for run 1, so the sensitivity version duplicates `perfect` there.
- *Fix:* report model-column format-only FPs; define format-only as "not in `actual_errors` and parses to the same number"; say what D6 is on nasa.

**M5. Inference: multiplicity, dependence and the tail are not stated.**
- 29 pairs × 3 models × 3 policies = 261 sign-flip tests plus 60 control tests at α = 0.05, uncorrected (≈ 13 false directions expected under a global null); 3 policies × 3 references × 2 degenerate treatments = 18 pooled permutation tests, none named primary.
- The 10 seed differences are not independent: two 80 % training sets share ≈ 64 % of the rows, so the exact sign-flip test, which assumes independent sign flips, is anti-conservative (Dietterich 1998; Nadeau & Bengio 2003). The test is still the right choice, but the plan calls it exact without this caveat.
- The permutation test is one-sided for "more concordant than chance". The misalignment the proposal asks for ("any discordant pair", §1) is shown pairwise, and "mostly discordant" cannot be certified by this test.
- *Fix:* name one primary test (reference (a), degenerates ranked last, per policy); call the pair verdicts and the other 17 tests descriptive; add the dependence caveat; say which tail is primary.

### Should-fix

**S1. §4 impute mechanism contradicts the review it claims to apply.** The plan says impute ranks by "cell-level precision on parsable errors"; review A8 says "recall on parsable errors, not cell precision". Neither alone: the net change is parsable TPs fixed (gain) minus parsable FPs overwritten (loss). Measured parsable TP / parsable FP in model columns: bc hand-written 138 / 0, Raha 74 / 75; nasa hand-written 298 / 0, Raha 349 / 1,864 (all format-only, so overwritten with an equal mean, no loss); bike Raha 28,852 / 3,636, hand-written 21,599 / 0. State "parsable TPs minus parsable FPs" and report both columns.

**S2. The plan misreports its own JSON.** §0 "small additions": adult `label_errors_left` under impute is 657–685 for Raha and 16–21 for HoloClean (`checks/measure_fixes.json` per-seed lists; identical to mine), not 658–685 and 18–20.

**S3. adult HoloClean under impute is near-degenerate but not flagged.** 1,102–1,153 rows kept, 144–164 minority rows, and 80.9–81.3 % of the feature cells in the kept rows are flagged, so most features are the mode/mean of ≤ 19 % of the values. Mark it in §5 and predict it in §9.

**S4. Primary reference (a) with model-column policies: sound as a pre-registration choice, but say what the bike disagreement is.** 2,648 of hand-written's 24,247 bike flags and 13,513 of Raha's 46,001 sit in excluded columns, and 3,537 of the 34,274 errors; the (a)-vs-(c) reversal (hand 0.829 > Raha 0.783 vs Raha 0.913 > hand 0.825) is an artefact of columns the model never sees. A discordant hand/Raha pair under (a) is therefore not a downstream finding; count that pair under (c) in the headline or report both side by side. (On bc the analogue is harmless: all 22 hand-written FPs and 14 of Raha's 89 FPs are in `Unnamed: 0`; the (a) and (c) orders agree.)

**S5. Random control (§8.6).** The test answers "do the flagged rows beat the same number of random rows", which is the right question, but a *tie* does not mean "wrong rows" where the removed errors are harmless (bc tree models, §9.5); read ties together with the GT-repair result. The control also ignores class balance on bc/adult. Note both in §8.6.

**S6. §10 item 5.** Grinsztajn et al. (2022) compares tree ensembles with deep networks on tabular data; it says nothing about reactions to data errors. CleanML (Li et al. 2021) is the citation for model-dependent cleaning effects. The "(nasa: DT 149, XGB 117, linear 10)" counts come from the 7 Oct report's figure counting and were not re-verified here.

**S7. §10 item 6** mixes datasets: Adult DT 0.17–0.99 vs Ridge 0.74–0.78 (p. 10); the DT-vs-GNB contrast is Breast Cancer (0.65–0.94 vs 0.15–0.85). Write both.

**S8. §7 check 1 on adult HoloClean.** The replay's 665,207 cells have never been compared with a real HoloClean run (the disk file, 341,890 cells, is from another constraint set). My independent implementation gives the identical set, and the counts match Table 3, so the risk is small, but if the regenerated file differs at cell level while matching counts, check 1 aborts. Decide now whether count identity suffices for adult (and note that HoloClean lower-cases and strips strings before matching, which the replay does not).

**S9. §8.4 permutation counts** (120/24/6) are for the ranked-last analysis. With degenerates left out under delete the items are bc 4, nasa 3, bike 3, adult 2 (24/6/6/2 permutations; adult has one pair). Say so; power is low there. My simulation of that design gives type I error 0.025–0.036.

That is two days of work, not one.
