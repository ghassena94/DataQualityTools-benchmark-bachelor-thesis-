mean DCs per column is what decides whether a leave-one-out ablation can say anything. Near 1, a constraint owns its columns outright, so removing it removes them and the gap it leaves is interpretable — that is the beers case. Well above 1, the same columns are named by several constraints, removing any one changes almost nothing, and the ablation is blind by construction.
